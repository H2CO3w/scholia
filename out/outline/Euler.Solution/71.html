71PacketStageInputs

Euler.PacketStageInputs

Actual packet inputs at every finite stage, together with the source-only parameter cap and the chosen frequency guard.

Packet

  • PacketFieldPhysicalSobolevEuler.PacketFieldPhysicalSobolev

    Fixed physical Sobolev bounds from the genuine all-order cylinder word bounds. The constants are finite polynomials at each fixed order; the oscillating phase costs only the indicated power of its frequency.

  • PacketGeometryInitialAmplitudeEuler.PacketGeometryInitialAmplitude

    The actual chosen primary amplitude has exponential initial decay. Its prefactor is a fixed polynomial in the same source parameters.

  • PacketInitialBoundsEuler.PacketInitialBounds

    Initial high and mean estimates retain their distinct small factors. The only truncation-dependent quantity is the already controlled tail base.

  • PacketInitialCostPolynomialEuler.PacketInitialCostPolynomial

    At each fixed Sobolev order, the two actual initial-increment costs are fixed polynomials in the source primitives. Frequency and amplitude are kept outside these polynomials.

  • PacketInitialInputEuler.PacketInitialInput

    Genuine parent/geometry inputs for an initial increment. The high and mean fields below are the actual solved profiles, not prescribed bounds.

  • PacketInitialPhysicalEuler.PacketInitialPhysical

    The high initial increment retains its small amplitude, while the mean initial increment is O(k⁻²) without any oscillatory-graph loss.

  • PacketInitialPolynomialBoundsEuler.PacketInitialPolynomialBounds

    Fixed-order source polynomial bounds for the literal initial increments. These use the same finite frequency guard as the constructed exact packet.

  • PacketInitialSmoothFieldsEuler.PacketInitialSmoothFields

    Ordinary smooth square-integrable fields realizing both actual initial increments, with the same concrete high and mean functions.

  • PacketInitializedInitialEuler.PacketInitializedInitial

    Source (22) for the literal initialized packet. The constants at each fixed Sobolev order are independent of its truncation and frequency.

  • PacketInitializedInitialExactEuler.PacketInitializedInitialExact

    The exact correction has zero initial value, so the two actual compactly supported initial increments are precisely the finite-packet high and mean fields whose physical Sobolev bounds were proved above.

  • PacketStageInputsEuler.PacketStageInputs

    Actual packet inputs at every finite stage, together with the source-only parameter cap and the chosen frequency guard.

  • PacketStagePhysicalBoundsEuler.PacketStagePhysicalBounds

    Physical estimates on the actual shortened parent state.

  • PacketUniformInitialBoundsEuler.PacketUniformInitialBounds

    The actual initial increments for the canonical uniformly selected packet satisfy source (22), with fixed-order polynomial costs.

Parent

  • ParentForwardNormalParametersEuler.ParentForwardNormalParameters

    The zero-history normal stage has the same fixed parameter envelope as every positive-history stage. Its actual initial coordinate has norm one.

  • ParentGeometryForwardChoiceEuler.ParentGeometryForwardChoice

    The actual zero-history geometry constructs the forward packet and its new smooth Euler state at the uniformly chosen frequency.

  • ParentGeometryInitialBoundsEuler.ParentGeometryInitialBounds

    Source-only initial estimates for the actual packet constructed from a parent and its activation geometry. No initial-field estimate is an input.
