9TransverseFrameCoordinates

Euler.TransverseFrameCoordinates

# Recovering the source's transverse coordinates The moving plane with normal `(F⁻¹)* m₀` is exactly the image under `F` of the fixed plane `m₀⊥`. Orthogonal projection gives a bounded coordinate map, and on the moving plane its reconstruction is the identity. These are coefficient identities, not assumptions about a differential inverse.
