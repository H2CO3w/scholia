18MeanStrongEstimates

Euler.MeanStrongEstimates

# Quantitative time estimates for the genuine mean inverse The constants depend explicitly and polynomially on the time interval, coefficient bounds, and the inverse-frame bound. The only square roots are the proved finite-time trace/Poincaré factors. These are estimates of the actual Bochner fields and continuous representatives constructed by the inverse.
