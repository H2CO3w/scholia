10TransverseVariationalInverse

Euler.TransverseVariationalInverse

# The actual zero-endpoint transverse displacement inverse We use the derivative of the physical displacement as the Hilbert-space variable. Terminal integration supplies the displacement. Its initial trace and its moving normal component are bounded linear constraints, hence define a closed Hilbert subspace. The kinetic energy is exactly the squared norm on this space; no norm equivalence or pre-existing differential inverse is assumed. This constructs the weak transverse inverse in source lines 172--184. The coordinate identity `η = F R ξ` and strong coordinate evolution require the separate frame and regularity arguments; they are not assumed in this file.
