25PrimaryTargetBounds

NavierStokes.PrimaryTargetBounds

# Actual primary covariance and the flat target The two columns use the two signs of the same representative and the actual Volterra primary. Target smallness at the attachment points is retained as a scalar factor throughout the finite matrix solve.

Mean

  • MeanChartCompatibilityNavierStokes.MeanChartCompatibility

    # Naturality of the actual mean operators Radial endpoints, the normalized density, the compactification cutoff, frequencies, and amplitudes are transported together. The identities below are identities of the defined integral/Fourier/rank operators, not an assumption that separately chosen chart outputs coincide.

  • MeanIncrementBoundsNavierStokes.MeanIncrementBounds

    # Changes of the literal mean equations under an actual mean increment The differential operators below are genuine graph derivatives. The nonlinear fields are the products in equation (32), including every old/new mean cross term. The unchanged wave covariance and virtual flux cancel only after an exact residual-difference identity.

  • MeanMomentBoundsNavierStokes.MeanMomentBounds

    # Actual slow moments of flat weighted mean fields The two-edge weight absorbs every fixed inverse-edge polynomial. Consequently the actual derivatives of a supported mean field have global band bounds. Finite torus averaging and radial integration preserve these bounds. All derivatives in this file are `iteratedFDeriv` of the actual integral.

  • MeanRankUpdateNavierStokes.MeanRankUpdate

    # The physical five-row mean update The update is the constructed power-moment inverse, transported with the physical length and velocity scales. Each moment carries its own scale.

  • MeanResidualNavierStokes.MeanResidual

    # Exact angularly averaged Navier--Stokes balances The average is a normalized actual interval integral. All coordinate derivatives are Fréchet derivatives on spacetime, and the Reynolds products include the entire oscillatory velocity.

Harmonic

  • HarmonicFieldsNavierStokes.HarmonicFields

    # Finite integer-harmonic fields The coefficients form a genuine finite group algebra. Evaluation is the literal exponential sum, multiplication is convolution, and angular means are actual interval integrals over a period of length `2*pi`.

  • HarmonicMeanInteractionNavierStokes.HarmonicMeanInteraction

    # Actual harmonic residual changes under a mean increment The wave and its pressure are held fixed. Every coefficient below belongs to the actual differential residual in `HarmonicResidual`; excluded errors are kept as separate additive differences.

  • HarmonicResidualNavierStokes.HarmonicResidual

    # Finite harmonic extraction of the actual cylindrical residual The coefficient operations below reconstruct genuine differential fields. Excluded errors remain explicit inputs with field-evaluation witnesses.

  • HarmonicWaveInteractionNavierStokes.HarmonicWaveInteraction

    # Actual harmonic wave-update interactions The nonlinear terms are finite convolutions of actual differentiated fields. All-jet classes are lifted and restricted by proved norm-one linear pullbacks before applying the full cylindrical divergence cancellation.

Other

Their affine derivatives, the native time scale, and adjacent-band changes give estimates whose constants precede the band, copy, and source. No native periodicity of the source is used.

  • CommonCoverSolveNavierStokes.CommonCoverSolve

  • CopyAngularInvarianceNavierStokes.CopyAngularInvariance

  • CopySolveCompatibilityNavierStokes.CopySolveCompatibility

    # Compatibility of the actual copy-path solve with a common-cover change The native slot is held fixed while the common cover is refined. Sources are pulled back along the covering map. Equality is proved first for the actual coefficient/forcing paths and then for the constructed Volterra inverse; no native periodicity of an inhomogeneous solution is assumed.

  • CorrectionStateNavierStokes.CorrectionState

Covariances are actual angular integrals, and the mean residuals are the differential expressions in `MeanIncrementBounds`.   • ErrorHarmonicsNavierStokes.ErrorHarmonics

  • IntegratedMeanBalancesNavierStokes.IntegratedMeanBalances

Compact radial support supplies the boundary cancellations, and parameter derivatives pass under integrals by the dominated differentiation theorem in `TransportPrimitive`.

  • LinearWaveBoundsNavierStokes.LinearWaveBounds
