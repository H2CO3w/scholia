32LabelSupportPreservation

NavierStokes.LabelSupportPreservation

# Preservation of the actual closed carrier of a harmonic label The support argument uses the whole native Volterra path. A pointwise zero of the source is not used as a zero of its particular solution. The Gaussian clock cutoff supplies the temporal boundary of the carrier; the source supplies its slow and transverse boundaries.
