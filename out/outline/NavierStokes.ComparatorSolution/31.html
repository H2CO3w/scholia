31ActualGaussianCoverage

NavierStokes.ActualGaussianCoverage

# Gaussian coverage for the actual scaled particular solves The cutoff used by the inverse is the separate Gaussian slot cutoff times the padded native-clock cutoff. The dyadic, radial, and slow source masks remain in the source and amplitude. Whole-path support, rather than a pointwise zero of the source, supplies zero germs for the actual Volterra solution.

Localized

  • LocalizedGaussianBoundsNavierStokes.LocalizedGaussianBounds

    # Gaussian cutoff errors from support-local primitive estimates The analytic phase patch may be smaller than the closed periodization cell. Only primitive amplitude/source/cutoff jets on that patch enter the Gaussian estimate. On the remainder of the cell, actual input zero germs imply a zero germ of the two-term cutoff error. The global source complement is retained once, exactly as in `CopyData.globalGaussian`.

  • LocalizedWaveBoundsNavierStokes.LocalizedWaveBounds

    # Wave bounds from primitive jets on native support cells The background phase and its material defect are controlled only where the native coefficient can be nonzero. No extension of these controls to the whole fast lift is required.
