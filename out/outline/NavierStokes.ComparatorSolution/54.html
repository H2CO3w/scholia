54PeriodicPaperTheorem

NavierStokes.PeriodicPaperTheorem

# The full periodic corollary of the paper The torus is represented by spatially periodic lifts to R³. The support clauses intersect topological support with the closed fundamental cube: they do not assert compact support of a nonzero periodic lift on all of R³.
