13RenormalizedHeatMoment

NavierStokes.RenormalizedHeatMoment

# The renormalized angular moment and physical axial viscosity The nonintegrable reference power is independent of physical axial position. It is subtracted before integration. Local constancy of the exterior heat carrier supplies compact support for every axial derivative of the difference.
